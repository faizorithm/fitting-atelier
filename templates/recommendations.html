How Fitting Atelier Chooses Your Outfit

Fitting Atelier uses a Hugging Face Sentence Transformer to convert your style request and wardrobe descriptions into numerical embeddings.

It calculates cosine similarity to compare their semantic meanings, then selects relevant clothing from different wardrobe categories.

A higher similarity score means the clothing description is more closely aligned with your request. These scores are not percentages, confidence levels, or probabilities.

The current outfit builder combines semantic similarity with category-based rules. It does not yet fully evaluate color coordination, fit, or occasion appropriateness.
